# Two Sum

Given *n* numbers and a target *k*, find the two numbers, at different
places, that add up to *k*.

## Input

A line with *n* and *k*, then a line with the *n* numbers. *n* is from 2
to 2,000; every number is between -1,000,000,000 and 1,000,000,000, and
exactly one pair adds up to *k*.

## Output

The places of the two numbers, counting from 1, smaller first, separated
by a space.

## Scoring

Each test is worth 20 points, all or nothing. The output is compared with
the answer word by word, so spacing does not matter.

## Example

Input:

    4 9
    2 7 11 15

Output:

    1 2

2 + 7 is 9.
