# Pairs

Given *n* numbers and a target *k*, find two of them, at different
places, that add up to *k*.

## Input

A line with *n* and *k*, then a line with the *n* numbers. *n* is at most
20,002; every number and *k* are between 1 and 2,000,000,000.

## Output

The places of the two numbers, counting from 1, separated by a space, or
`-1` when no two add up to *k*. Any right pair is accepted.

## Example

Input:

    4 9
    3 8 1 5

Output:

    2 3
